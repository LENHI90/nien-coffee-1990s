## Brand & Style

This design system evokes the soul of 1990s urban Vietnam—where slow-drip phin coffee, aged teak furniture, yellowed book pages, and faded stamp marks frame everyday life. The visual identity bridges the romantic lyricism of old Saigon/Hanoi coffee culture with a tactile, skeuomorphic, and editorial warmth.

The target audience comprises culture enthusiasts, vintage admirers, and specialty coffee lovers seeking solace in a fast-paced digital era. The emotional tone is contemplative, slow, artisanal, and deeply nostalgic ("Một ngụm cà phê – Một mảnh ký ức").

Aesthetic direction:
- **Tactile & Skeuomorphic Heritage:** Surfaces resemble aged handmade dó paper, unvarnished walnut counters, typewriter receipt bills, and brass-trimmed measuring instruments.
- **Micro-Texture Over Gloss:** Digital elements are grounded in physical imperfections—subtle fiber grains, postmark ink bleeding, perforated stamp margins, and antique paper edges.
- **Editorial Elegance:** Broad editorial margins paired with expressive, high-contrast serif headlines that echo mid-century Vietnamese literary journals.

## Colors

The palette reproduces the tactile organic warmth of a sun-faded street corner in 1990s Vietnam. 

### Role Mapping
- **Canvas & Surface Tones:**
  - `Base Surface` (`#F5EBE1`): Warm parchment paper; the primary canvas tone for views and screens.
  - `Raised Surface` (`#FAF5EE`): Vintage cream; used for stacked cards, paper bills, and focal content cards.
  - `Sunken / Aged Surface` (`#EFE3D3`): Aged tea paper tint; applied to input fields, table footers, and passive containers.
- **Primary & Structural Neutrals:**
  - `Primary Walnut` (`#4A3525`): Used for primary CTAs, main borders, key headlines, and prominent navigation headers.
  - `Deep Espresso` (`#2B170E`): Grounding dark tone for high-contrast reading text, heavy graphic rules, and fine print.
  - `Antique Bronze` (`#8C6D46`): Supporting muted neutral for secondary outlines, divider ornaments, and inactive icons.
- **Accents:**
  - `Wax Seal / Postmark Red` (`#B53323`): Expressive accent for urgent tags, wax seal stamp badges, active notifications, and pricing accents.
  - `Moss Green` (`#234E36`): Herbaceous accent for origin badges, organic farm certifications, status confirmations, and subtle secondary badges.
  - `Mustard Ochre` (`#C98B2C`): Warm highlight for star ratings, golden hour notices, and curated collection banners.

## Typography

Typography establishes an intimate, literary atmosphere with clear contextual role-splitting:

- **Headlines (`Playfair Display`):** Carries the classic editorial romanticism of colonial-era prints and antique café signboards. Vietnamese tonal diacritics sit naturally with generous ascender clearance.
- **Body (`Be Vietnam Pro`):** Engineered natively for Vietnamese glyphs and diacritics. Provides crisp, unstrained readability across long tasting notes, origins, and stories.
- **Data & Mechanical Accents (`Courier Prime`):** Simulates the ink impression of an Olympia mechanical typewriter or manual cash register. Used strictly for prices, order numbers, roast dates, extraction timers, and receipt stamps.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy mirrors a vintage scrapbook or a curated coffee journal, using generous interior margins and structured grids that never feel rigid or clinical.

- **Grid Model:**
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid layout with `margin: 1rem` and `gutter: 1.25rem`. Elements stack primarily in a single vertical register reminiscent of a fold-out pocket menu.
  - **Tablet (768px – 1024px):** 8-column fluid grid with `margin: 2rem`. Offers asymmetric 5/3 content-sidebar splits.
  - **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column layout capped at a maximum width of `1280px` with `margin: 3.5rem` and `gutter: 2rem`. Content surfaces are centered with wide, deliberate side margins.
- **Vertical Rhythm:**
  - Component padding utilizes strict steps (`space-sm` to `space-xl`) to maintain a relaxed, breathable cadence that discourages rushed interaction.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth does not rely on synthetic blurred drop shadows; instead, it utilizes physical layering, paper overlapping, and subtle atmospheric cast shadows that simulate directional natural light passing through wood louvers.

- **Level 0 (Flat Paper Bed):** `#F5EBE1` canvas background with a 3% opacity film grain texture overlay.
- **Level 1 (Stacked Ephemera & Recipe Slips):** `#FAF5EE` card surfaces sitting on top of the bed, outlined by a 1px solid border in `#8C6D46` at 25% opacity, paired with an offset ambient shadow: `0 2px 8px rgba(43, 23, 14, 0.08)`.
- **Level 2 (Active Cards, Stamp Badges & Sticky Notes):** Angled surfaces (rotated -0.5deg to 1deg). Elevated by `0 6px 16px rgba(43, 23, 14, 0.12)` with a slight warm brown cast.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Wax-Sealed Receipt Overlays):** Deep layered elevation: `0 16px 32px rgba(43, 23, 14, 0.22)`. Accompanied by a background dimming wash using `#2B170E` at 60% opacity with a sepia-toned backdrop filter.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a grounded, hand-cut paper feel, primarily using slight softened corners (`roundedness: 1`, base `4px`) to emulate card stock cut by manual guillotines rather than smoothed digital pills.

- **Standard Containers:** `rounded: 0.25rem` (4px) gives structured stability to receipts, cards, and text boxes.
- **Specialized Stamp Cutouts:** Postage elements feature repetitive serrated semi-circular mask notches along their borders (radius `4px` at `10px` intervals).
- **Wax Seals & Circular Postmarks:** Pure geometric circles (`rounded-full`) stamped with intentional off-center rotation and irregular edge masks.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Walnut Plank):** Background in `#4A3525`, typography in `#FAF5EE`, subtle inner 1px border in `#8C6D46` (40% opacity). Hover shifts background to `#2B170E` with a slight tactile drop shadow.
- **Secondary Action (Wax Red Accent):** Background in `#B53323`, typography in `#FAF5EE`. For urgent cart triggers or order finalization.
- **Tertiary / Outline (Brass Wire):** 1px border in `#8C6D46`, transparent background, text in `#4A3525`. Hover adds `#EFE3D3` tint.

### Postage Stamp Chips & Badges
- Filter tags and categories are styled as miniature postal stamps: cream paper surface (`#FAF5EE`), perforated zigzag border mask, with bold uppercase `Courier Prime` labels.
- Selected state turns the chip into an inked postmark with a double-ring outline in `#B53323` or `#234E36`.

### Cards (Menu Items & Origins)
- Constructed like aged postcard photographs: a top sepia-toned image container with a subtle inner vignette (`box-shadow: inset 0 0 20px rgba(43,23,14,0.3)`), supported by a cream paper footer (`#FAF5EE`) containing serif title, typewriter pricing (`Courier Prime`), and tasting notes.
- Optional kraft paper badge anchored to the top-right corner via a brass-colored grommet visual anchor.

### Form Inputs
- Designed as ledger lines: background set to `#EFE3D3`, bottom border 1.5px solid `#8C6D46`, side and top borders transparent.
- Typed text displays in `Be Vietnam Pro` in `#2B170E`, while placeholder text uses `Courier Prime` in `#8C6D46` at 70% opacity. Focus state deepens bottom border to `#4A3525`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Custom square box with 1.5px solid `#4A3525` border on `#FAF5EE`.
- Checked state fills with a hand-drawn checkmark or cross mark reminiscent of red grease pencil (`#B53323`). Radio buttons use a filled concentric wax-seal circle.

### Lists & Receipts
- Menu and billing breakdowns use dotted ledger leader lines (`. . . . .`) between the Vietnamese item name (left, serif) and typewriter price (right, `Courier Prime`).
- Completed receipts feature a faint 15-degree tilted red postmark seal reading "ĐÃ THANH TOÁN" (PAID).
